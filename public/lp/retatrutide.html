How it works

Triple receptor action — nothing else comes close

Retatrutide is the first peptide to simultaneously activate three metabolic receptors, delivering superior fat loss and metabolic control compared to dual or single agonists.

GLP-1 Receptor
Reduces appetite, slows gastric emptying, improves insulin sensitivity. The same pathway targeted by Ozempic and Wegovy.

Retatrutide is a triple GLP-1 agonist targeting GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. Ozempic and Wegovy (semaglutide) target only the GLP-1 receptor. This triple mechanism produced significantly greater weight loss in Phase 3 clinical trials — 28.3% vs 13.7% body weight reduction.
